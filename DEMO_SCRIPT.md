# SIH demo script (≈7 minutes)

1. **Intro + login** – watch the AICTE NEXUS intro. Show the Light/Dark switch on the login page. Sign in as `senior.anita` / `Nexus@2026`.
2. **Command Center** – alert strip (overdue, due soon, critical), KPIs, charts. Flip to 🌙 Dark mode: everything follows.
3. **Role-based access** – sign out, sign in as `finance.suresh` (Bureau Officer): only Finance complaints, no Settings/User Management.
   Try *Update status → Closed*: not offered. (Closing is a senior decision.)
4. **Register + track** – *Complaint Management → New complaint*, note the generated ID, open *Track a complaint* and show the stepper.
5. **Resolve → Reopen** – resolve a Finance case, then *Reopen → Submit reopen request*. Sign in as `senior.anita` → *Reopen requests* → approve. Show due-date reset.
6. **Cross-Bureau Intelligence** – open a High suggestion, read the reasons, **Verify** it (Senior) – nothing merges automatically.
7. **Risk + alerts** – Risk Monitoring: overdue, due-soon, recommended actions.
8. **Analytics** – apply filters, download CSV and the **PDF** summary.
9. **NEXUS AI** – ask "Summarise the most overdue complaint"; point out the privacy mode (nothing leaves the server unless switched on).
10. **Audit trail** – filter by the complaint ID; click **Verify audit-log integrity**. Mention every login, export and decision is logged.
11. **Admin** – sign in as `admin`: User Management (create user, reset password), Settings → Create backup.
